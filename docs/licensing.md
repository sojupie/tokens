## Material Design 3 data

The hosted collection comes from Google's Material Design 3 source payloads.
The capture preserves the payloads and provenance; publication groups their
token values into tables and exports. Captured payloads and derived token values retain their upstream terms.

Google's [Material Design licensing statement](https://m3.material.io/get-started)
identifies Apache 2.0 or CC BY 4.0 for its content and resources. Keep applicable
item-specific notices when redistributing captured or derived data. Check the terms for the specific material you distribute.

[Apache 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) and
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) have their own notice
and attribution requirements. Include the applicable license and attribution
with a redistributed data set, identify your transformations, and preserve
upstream notices. BSL 1.1 applies to the software engine that performs those transformations.

Source URLs, capture dates and hashes establish where a value came from. They
do not establish which license applies. Check the source's terms and any
item-specific notices when preparing a data distribution.

## Distribution

Keep `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` with source distributions. Preserve
bundled font, icon and JavaScript licenses. Binary distributions must also
retain applicable notices from linked dependencies.

For a captured or derived data distribution, include its source attribution,
applicable upstream terms and a description of the transformation. The current
JSON downloads do not include a complete license notice in each response.
Include that information alongside the JSON when redistributing it.

Token Catalog does not claim Google's trademarks or endorsement. Source names
are used to identify the collection and its provenance.

## Other collections

A new design system needs a recorded source, capture method and license before
its data is distributed. Record those separately from the BSL 1.1 license for the
catalog software. Update the notices when adding copied code, fonts, icons,
examples or other assets from a new publisher.
